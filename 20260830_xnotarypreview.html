How xNotary works

A simple evidence layer for documents — without sending the document to us.

From file to verifiable proof

Three steps. The original document remains under your control.

01 / Fingerprint

Your browser reads the file

It creates a unique fingerprint. The file itself does not leave your device.

02 / Record

xNotary records the proof

The fingerprint, time and any signatures are bound together in a certificate.

03 / Verify

Anyone can check it

The original file and certificate can be verified later, independently.

What xNotary proves

  • The exact file fingerprint
  • The recorded time
  • Signatures bound to that fingerprint
  • Whether the certificate still verifies

What xNotary does not prove — today

  • That statements inside the document are true
  • A signer’s legal authority unless separately verified
  • Official government or civil-law notarisation
  • Enforceability in every jurisdiction
